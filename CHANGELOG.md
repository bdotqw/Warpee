# Warpee

## 1.6.0

New

- Recent, a row above the favorites that holds whatever came into your bags this session. Each arrival takes the first free cell, the oldest one leaves when the row is full, and the row is always as wide as the grid. The number on a cell counts only what arrived, and it falls as you use the delivery up. A cross on the right empties the row by hand, and the row clears itself on logout or a reload. Right click uses the item, counting is frozen at a banker, and anything already pinned in the favorites stays out of the row. Turn it off in the settings, under Favorites
- The bag header names its slot counter again, Slots 303/374, and the bank and the warband bank name theirs the same way
- Hide reagents, a new setting in the bags grid: the reagent bag is left out of the window, its slots keep counting in the header, and reagents still go into it

Fixes

- A stack that comes out of a container you just opened, a supply pouch for instance, is counted on a favorite right away instead of after the next bag change
- The description of a dropdown in the settings opens above the row while the list is closed, and follows the cell you hover on the right once the list is open
- The Russian tooltip on the clean up button read as scrapping rather than sorting, and the Spanish one carried an article the game does not use
