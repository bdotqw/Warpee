# Warpee

## 1.5.1

New

- Dragging a favorite plays the item's own pickup and drop sound, the one the game uses for that kind of item. A favorite whose item is not in your bags falls back to the plain cursor sounds
- The number on a favorite counts every stack of that item you carry, so 20 potions in one bag and 5 in another read as 25. Only items that stack are added up, and the bank stays out of it
