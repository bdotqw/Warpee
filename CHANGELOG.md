# Warpee

## 2.1.0

New

- Italian and Brazilian Portuguese
- The plus on the pocket opens a quick add window of popular items; a click pins one into the pocket or takes it out, and crafted ones wear their quality tier
- The bank runs on the game's own tab strip, and a right click edits a tab's name and icon with the full Blizzard icon set in a scrollable grid, or a FileID and an item link
- Fourteen themes, pruned from over twenty and tuned so every border, line and accent reads on its surface
- Settings grouped into collapsible sections: Quick access, Pocket size and Bag arrangement, with a tidier General and Vendor
- The favorites row always spans the grid; the how many slots slider is gone
- A red border can mark unwearable gear
- The pocket window takes a local name for a small bag once it grows wide

Fixes

- Search fades now cover slot badges, quality and reagent rings and crafted quality corners, in bags, bank, favorites and recent
- Switching themes leaves no stuck art behind
- Recent rows no longer fill with old loot after a key and a chest
- A pasted icon link applies to a bank tab on Save
- The coin counter no longer crashes
- The repair dropdown reads naturally in Russian and German
