# Warpee

## 5.0

New

- The pocket carries a lock of its own: a padlock in its header and the same switch under the Pocket tab. The lock in the settings covers the bags and the bank now, and nothing else
- Four arrows along the bottom edge nudge the pocket while it is unlocked, and pinning it no longer changes its size
- The reagent bag has a switch in the bag header, beside the character list: it takes the reagent block out of the window and puts it back, while its slots keep counting in the header
- The recent row has a switch per window: one for the bags and one for the pocket, over the one list
- The recent row takes a modified left click again: Ctrl + left click opens the dressing room and Shift + left click puts the link in chat, in the bag window and in the pocket alike. A plain left click still takes nothing

Fixes

- A cell of the recent row leaves with its item: equip or use the last one and the cell and its Clear button go together, instead of staying behind
- The bank window stays in step with the game's own tab while the banker is open, so coming back to the warband bank no longer leaves the window on the other one: the search dim marks the grid you are looking at, and a right click deposits into that same bank
- A slot that is emptied stops carrying the search dim
- The recent and favorite rows keep a single separator from the grid when the reagent block is drawn above it

## 4.0

Fixes

- The character picker no longer errors on open

## 3.0

New

- The bank tab editor is the game's own: a right click opens the icon list, the tab name and the auto-deposit settings
- A bank the realm has switched off says so instead of looking empty
- Auto-sell never touches pets, augment runes, alchemist stones, profession items, fishing gear, fun trinkets or twink gear

Fixes

- Renaming a bank tab and changing its icon apply again, and the menu opens clear of the tab strip
- The bank cleanup button sorts straight away, with no confirmation popup
- The guild bank and the icon popup lost the second frame around them
- A profile name that is taken is refused, and a broken import no longer breaks the login
- Vault counters, the quest marker and the recent row stop going stale
- The pocket shortcut line is translated
